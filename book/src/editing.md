# Entering and editing tab

- Click a beat (or use the arrow keys) and type a fret number; two digits typed quickly make 10–36.
- ↑/↓ move between strings; ←/→ between beats. Moving past the end of a bar adds beats and bars.
- Set durations from the palette, the Note menu, `+`/`−`, or Alt+1…7. `.` dots, `/` makes a triplet.
- Techniques use Guitar Pro's keys: `P` palm mute, `H` hammer-on/pull-off, `S` slide, `B` bend,
  `V` vibrato, `X` dead note, `G` ghost note, `A` accent, `I` let ring, `L` tie, `R` rest.
- Select with Shift+arrows, a drag or Shift+click; ⌘C, ⌘X, ⌘V copy, cut and paste; ⌘↑/⌘↓ transpose.
  Techniques and dynamics apply to everything selected.
- Click a fret on the fretboard to put that note under the cursor.
- **Note › Grace Note…** adds a small quick note before the one under the cursor: pick its fret and
  length, whether it comes before the beat or on it, and whether it slides, bends or hammers into
  the note. **Note › Trill…** and **Note › Tremolo Picking…** work the same way.
- **Tools › Chord Diagrams…** puts a chord name and diagram over the beat under the cursor. It
  starts from the notes you entered and names them (E5, Am7, D/F#); click the frets to change the
  shape, above the nut to switch a string between open and not played, and **Name it** to rename.

## Drums

Add a kit with **Add drums** in the Track inspector. On a drum track the bottom panel becomes a drum
pad: click a pad to add or remove that hit at the cursor. Or type the General MIDI number as two
digits, as in Guitar Pro: 36 kick, 38 snare, 42 closed hi-hat, 46 open hi-hat, 49 crash, 51 ride,
52 china, 43 floor tom.
