# Introduction

Guitario writes, reads and plays guitar and bass tablature with standard notation, in the way
Guitar Pro does: the same layout, menus and keys. It is one of the sibling apps from the
[Guitario](https://github.com/timothydaly1/guitario) team: free, open source, written in Rust, and fully drivable by
scripts and agents.

Guitario is in **pre-alpha**: see [the roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) for what works and what does not.
