# Playback and practice

**Space** plays from the cursor and stops. The LCD turns on a one-bar count-in (hourglass) and the
metronome; beside it are Loop, the relative speed (25–200 %, pitch unchanged) and the relative
tonality (up to an octave up or down, for playing along in another key; drums and the score are
unchanged, click the number to reset). With a selection and Loop on, Space
loops just the selection. Mute and solo tracks in the global view.

Guitario's instruments are modelled plucked strings with an amp model for overdriven tones, and a
synthesised General MIDI drum kit.
