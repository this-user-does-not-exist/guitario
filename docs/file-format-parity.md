# File format parity

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-17 · **Last updated:** 2026-10-19 · **Change:** minor (a string section's players in `.gt` and MusicXML `<ensemble>`) · **Target:** Guitar Pro 8

Guitar Pro 8's file formats, and what Guitario does with each. Part of
[target-app parity](target-app-parity.md); gaps G2 and G3 in [gaps](gaps.md).

| Format | Guitar Pro 8 | Guitario | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.gp` (Guitar Pro 7/8: ZIP + `score.gpif`) | read, write | **read (partial), write** | `crates/gp`. Reads song information (title, subtitle, artist, album, words, music or words-and-music, copyright, transcriber, notice, instructions), tracks with their short names, colours, audio engine (`AudioEngineState` RSE or MIDI), interpretation (`AutoBrush`, `PalmMute`, `AutoAccentuation`, `PlayingStyle`, `UseOneChannelPerString`; automatic let ring has no known tag and stays in `.gt`) and notations (standard, tab, slash, numbered from `Content/PartConfiguration`, also written), tunings, capo, programs, bars, time/key signatures, repeats, sections, tempo, rhythms with dots and tuplets, dynamics, notes and their effects; shared objects; reads drum kits (by articulation index or MIDI number); reads up to four voices per bar, alternate endings and directions; reads grace beats as the grace notes of the next beat's notes (a grace note with no struck note after it on its string is dropped with a warning); reads chord names and diagrams (the staff's `DiagramCollection`, frets from each diagram's `baseFret`); reads lyrics (the lines as typed, or rebuilt from the beats' syllables when those are empty) fermatas (type, offset in quarter notes, length), fingering (`LeftFingering`, `RightFingering`), whammy curves (Guitar Pro 7/8's `<Whammy>` element, Guitar Pro 6's `WhammyBar…` properties; `<WhammyExtend/>` is not read yet) and vibrato with the bar (`VibratoWTremBar`), all written back. Fixture: `demos/guitar-pro/import-test.gp`. **Export** (`writegp.rs`, File › Export › Guitar Pro 8…) writes the same model back: up to four voices, ties and hammer-ons marked at both ends, every effect, grace notes (as grace beats before their beat, one per kind), chord names and diagrams (one `DiagramCollection` item per distinct chord), lyrics (the lines and each beat's syllable), fermatas, fingering, repeats, endings, directions, drum kits; track volume/pan and chords on drum tracks are left out with a warning. It reads back unchanged for every demo and all 76 real files tried, but has **not been opened in Guitar Pro itself** yet (human check) |
| `.gpx` (Guitar Pro 6) | read | **read (partial)** | `crates/gp/src/gpx.rs`: the BCFZ-compressed BCFS container around the same GPIF XML, with Guitar Pro 6's track layout (tuning and capo on the track, `GeneralMidi` programs). 37 real files import (local corpus). Drum hits stored as kit element and variation are read (kick, snare hit, rim and side stick, cowbell, five toms, hi-hat closed, half and open, pedal hi-hat, two crashes, splash, ride middle, edge and bell, china: matched against Guitar Pro 5 copies of the same files; two unseen elements are dropped with a warning); non-fretted tracks (voice, winds, keys) are skipped |
| `.gp5` / `.gp4` / `.gp3` | read (and `.gp5` export) | **read, `.gp5` write** | `crates/gp/src/legacy.rs` reads the binary formats: song information (all ten fields; GP3/4's one author field is words and music), tracks with their colours, notations (GP5 display flags: tablature, standard) and up to seven strings and their MIDI channels, two voices (GP5), bars with repeats, alternate endings (GP3/4 numbers and GP5 bit sets), markers, keys, triplet feel, GP5 directions, tempo changes from mix tables, chords, beat text, strokes, slap/pop/tapping, fades in and every note effect the model holds. Tremolo picking (one byte per note, kept per beat), trills (the other fret and a period) and grace notes (fret, transition, length, and in GP5 the dead and on-beat flags) are read and written; lyrics (one track's five lines in GP4-5; a second track's are left out of a `.gp5` with a warning) are read and written; fingering (two bytes behind note flag 0x80) and beam groupings ("beam eighth notes by", written with each time signature and kept by the bars after it) are read and written; bends are read and written with every point (GP3-5: all; GP7/8: origin, plateau and destination), whammy dives are read (Guitar Pro 3 keeps one depth, read as a dip; 4-5 the whole curve) and written to `.gp5`, vibrato with the bar is left out of a `.gp5` with a warning, and a `.gp5` export reports fermatas it cannot hold. `crates/gp/src/write5.rs` writes 5.10 files (**File › Export › Guitar Pro 5…**). 39 real GP3-5 files import and come back unchanged through the writer; every demo round-trips (`crates/gp/tests/gp5.rs`, `demos/gp5/`) |
| `.gtp`, PowerTab `.ptb`, TablEdit `.tef` | read | missing | |
| MusicXML | read, write | **read, write** | `crates/musicxml`. Export (File › Export › MusicXML…, `file.exportMusicXml`): MusicXML 4.0 partwise, one part per track with its MIDI program, volume and pan (and a string section's players as `<ensemble>`); the tuning and capo in `<staff-details>` and each note's string and fret in `<technical>`, so another program keeps the tab; a G or F clef an octave down (or a TAB clef for tab-only tracks), up to four voices joined by `<backup>`, tuplets with brackets, ties, repeats with their counts, alternate endings, double bars, sections (rehearsal letter and name), tempo marks, segno, coda, Fine and jumps, triplet feel as swing, keys, dynamics, beat text, chord names (root, kind, bass) and diagrams (`<frame>`), lyrics with syllabic, fermatas; drums as unpitched notes on channel 10 with an instrument per kit piece; hammer-ons and pull-offs (with a slur), shift and legato slides, slides in and out (scoop, plop, doit, falloff), bends of every Guitar Pro type, harmonics, vibrato and wide vibrato (SMuFL guitar vibrato), trills, tremolo picking, palm mute, let ring, ghost and dead notes, accents, staccato, tapping, fingering for both hands, pick strokes, brushes, grace notes. Not written: whammy curves, vibrato with the bar, fades (reported). Import (File › Import › MusicXML…, File › Open on `.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`): the same, plus UTF-16 files, `<transpose>`, pickup bars, and parts that repeat a tab staff on a notation staff (read from the tab). Every demo comes back bar for bar; MuseScore 4 reads the export, and its own MusicXML of a demo imports with the same rhythms and pitches (`crates/musicxml/tests/data`). Timewise files are refused |
| MIDI | read, write | **read, write** | `crates/midi`. Export: format 1, repeats unrolled, ties merged, mute/solo respected, with the title, tempo, time and key signatures; harmonics at the pitch they sound (a semi-harmonic with the note under it), staccato at half length. Bends, slides and the whammy bar are not written as pitch bends yet. Import (`import.rs`, File › Import › MIDI…, or File › Open on a `.mid`): format 0 and 1 (2 read as one song); a track per MIDI track and channel with its name, program, volume and pan; channel 10 as a drum kit on the kit lanes; tempo, time and key signatures as bar properties; note starts quantized per quarter note to the coarsest grid that holds them (quarters down to 32nds, eighth and sixteenth triplets; played timing falls back to sixteenths or eighth triplets); chords fingered near the hand without crossing strings, in a tuning chosen from the range (standard, drop D, 7 or 8 strings, 4 or 5-string bass), notes out of range moved by octaves. Not read: pitch bends, tempo changes inside a bar, controller changes after the first note (each reported). Every demo exported to MIDI comes back with 99-100 % of its notes at the same moment |
| PDF | write | **write** | `crates/ui-egui/src/pdf.rs`: File › Export › PDF… and `guitario-cli convert in out.pdf`. Vector pages on the score's paper (twelve formats or a custom size; portrait or landscape; four margins) from the page view's own engraving, with the stylesheet's headers and footers, embedded font subsets, selectable text, one track or all, every bar or a range, with or without notation; each track starts a page. Image export and the other stylesheet tabs are missing (G2, G8) |
| Print | yes | **yes** | File › Print… (Cmd+P) and `file.print`: printer, copies, collate, sides, black and white, through craft-print (CUPS, native Windows); pages shrink to fit other paper. File › Print Preview… shows the pages as the PDF has them, File › Stylesheet… sets the paper, headers and footers, and Print can take only the selected bars; not yet tried on a real printer (human check) |
| WAV | write | **write** | `crates/playback` offline render, 16-bit stereo |
| MP3, FLAC, OGG | write | missing | |
| PNG / SVG images | write | missing | |
| ASCII tab | write (import too) | **write** | `crates/engine/src/ascii.rs` |
| Native | `.gp` | `.gt` | Versioned JSON, validated on load, atomic saves via craft-atomic. A track's `players` (a string section's size, which Guitar Pro has no place for) is kept only here and in MusicXML |

| 2026-10-16 | minor | Tremolo picking and trills read and written in `.gp5` (checked on the real corpus: no longer dropped) and in `.gp`/GPIF (`<Tremolo>` 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and `<Trill>` as a pitch; inferred from real files and one sample, not yet opened in Guitar Pro 8) |
